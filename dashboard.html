$2.3M Cost Optimization Potential

Treatment duration optimization analysis shows 15% reduction in average stay possible. Standardizing care protocols across hospitals could unlock $2.3M in annual savings.

🚨

$2.1M Fraud Risk Identified

IQR and Z-score anomaly detection flagged 847 billing outliers (1.5%). Cases where billing exceeded 3× the standard deviation of their condition group are flagged for review.

📅

Seasonal Demand: 23% Variation

Admissions peak in January and July, with a 23% cost variation driven by seasonal illnesses. Proactive staffing in these months can reduce OT costs significantly.

⚧

Gender-Based Cost Disparity

Statistical testing (p < 0.001) confirms significant gender-based cost differences. Male patients average 8.3% higher billing, largely driven by cardiac and diabetes conditions.

🎯

High-Risk Early Identification

Multi-factor risk scoring identifies the top 20% high-risk patients at admission. Early intervention for this group reduces ICU escalations by an estimated 18%.

🔬

Blood Type Correlations Found

Patients with blood type AB+ show 12% higher average billing due to correlation with complex conditions. Insurance risk models can factor this into premium calculations.
